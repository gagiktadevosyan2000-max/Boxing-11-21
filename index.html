Boxing Champions

Anthony Joshua

Anthony Joshua is a famous British boxer. He started boxing when he was 18 years old. In 2012, he won a gold medal at the Olympic Games in London. In 2013, he became a professional boxer. He won several world heavyweight titles and became one of the most famous boxers in the world. He fought against many famous boxers, including Wladimir Klitschko, Andy Ruiz Jr., and Oleksandr Usyk. One of his most famous fights was against Wladimir Klitschko in 2017. Joshua won the fight by knockout in the 11th round.
